Voice Center

The human voice is essential for communication, professional performance, and quality of life. Dr. Luke Pasick specializes in the evaluation and treatment of voice disorders affecting professional voice users as well as individuals experiencing changes in their speaking or singing voice.

Evaluation & Treatment

Each patient receives an individualized assessment and treatment plan. Advanced diagnostic tools and treatment options may include:

  • Laryngeal Videostroboscopy
  • Voice Therapy
  • Office-Based Procedures
  • Microlaryngeal Surgery
  • Treatment of Vocal Fold Paralysis
  • Treatment of Benign Vocal Fold Lesions
