About Dr. Pasick

Dr. Luke Pasick specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of disorders affecting the voice, swallowing, and upper airway. He completed advanced fellowship training in Laryngology at Columbia University Irving Medical Center and NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital following residency training at the University of Miami.
