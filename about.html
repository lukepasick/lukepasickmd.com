About Luke J. Pasick, MD

Luke Pasick, MD is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Otolaryngology - Head and Neck Surgery, Division of Laryngology at the University of Miami Miller School of Medicine. A fellowship-trained laryngologist, Dr. Pasick specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of throat disorders, including those affecting the voice, upper airway, and swallowing. As part of the Division of Laryngology at UHealth, he collaborates closely with a multidisciplinary team of physicians and highly specialized speech-language pathologists to provide comprehensive, patient-centered care.

Originally from the northeast, Dr. Pasick earned his medical degree from Drexel University College of Medicine, where he was awarded the Award for Outstanding Community Service and the Student Achievement Award in Medical Genetics. He was inducted into the Pathology Honor Society and Alpha Omega Alpha Honor Medical Society. Under the mentorship of Dr. Robert Sataloff during medical school, Dr. Pasick developed an early interest in laryngology as a gateway to helping improve patients’ quality of life and ability to communicate with ease. He then completed his residency training in Otolaryngology - Head and Neck Surgery at University of Miami/Jackson Health System. As Chief Resident, he was honored by his coresidents with the Otolaryngology Resident Teaching Award.

Following residency, Dr. Pasick pursued fellowship training in Laryngology at the Columbia University Center for Voice and Swallowing and the Weill Cornell Medicine Sean Parker Institute for the Voice. During fellowship, he gained extensive experience caring for professional voice users including: performers, teachers, and lawyers, as well as patients with complex swallowing and airway disorders. Throughout training, he noticed a great need for improving the quality of life for survivors of head and neck cancer, which is a focus in his practice. He enjoys providing specialized care for patients across the breadth of laryngology and remains active in clinical research, having presented his work at national and international scientific meetings.
