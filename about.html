About Dr. Luke Pasick

Dr. Luke Pasick is a fellowship-trained laryngologist specializing in the diagnosis and treatment of voice, swallowing, and airway disorders.

Following residency training in Otolaryngology–Head and Neck Surgery at the University of Miami Miller School of Medicine and Jackson Memorial Hospital, Dr. Pasick completed advanced fellowship training in Laryngology at Columbia University Center for Voice & Swallowing and Weil Cornell Medicine Sean Parker Institute for the Voice in New York City. He takes great pride in caring for patients with vocal fold cancer using the transoral laser microsurgery technique. Dr. Pasick also treats those afflicted with the side effects of radiation following head and neck cancer treatment, mainly for swallowing (dysphagia) or voice production.

Dr. Pasick currently serves as Assistant Professor of Clinical Otolaryngology at the University of Miami Miller School of Medicine and provides specialized care through UHealth, the University of Miami Health System.
